Our forward deployed engineers (FDEs) help define, implement, and test your circuit breakers. Sales engineering is included in your Phylax subscription. You can also [write your own policies](./writing-your-own-policies).

For product fit, see [Should you use Phylax?](./should-you-use-phylax).

## Start a Conversation

[Contact us through our website](https://phylax.systems) or your Phylax contact. Our first meeting covers your protocol, the scenarios that concern you, and existing mitigations.

Use the [preparation guide](./evaluate-credible-layer) to gather the context.

## The Integration Process

When you work with our forward deployed engineers, here's what you get.

| Stage | What our FDEs deliver | What your team reviews or authorizes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scope protection | Study your contracts, assets, critical operations, existing mitigations, and failure scenarios. | The protected operations, legitimate exceptions, and coverage gaps. |
| Define policies | Translate agreed scenarios into policies, thresholds, and recovery behavior. | Intended policy behavior and acceptable exceptions. |
| Implement and validate | Write assertions, run backtests, test a replica of your protocol on an Ethereum fork, and investigate false positives. | Assertion code and evidence for legitimate, violating, and exceptional activity. |
| Integrate enforcement | Prepare tested contract guards, transaction-routing changes, and recovery procedures. | Protocol changes, customer-controlled signing, and integration evidence. |
| Activate and operate | Prepare and simulate activation transactions, verify live protection, and set up the agreed monitoring and response process. | Production authorization and execution by your signers, plus the operating plan. |

We agree the delivery scope and operating plan with your team.

## Who Does What

Your team explains protocol behavior, identifies acceptable exceptions, and approves the policies and test results. Involve the people responsible for contract changes, transaction submission, governance, and incident response.

Your team controls its policies and protocol. Our FDEs prepare and simulate changes. Your signers authorize and execute them. See [Managing Policy Deployments](./managing-policy-deployments) for deployment permissions.

## Before you activate your first circuit breaker

Our FDEs run [backtests](./backtesting) and test a replica of your protocol on an Ethereum fork. Review normal activity, policy violations, legitimate exceptions, and recovery behavior with us.

The [testing strategy](./testing-strategy) covers guards and transaction routing. After your signers activate protection, we verify it with you.

## Fallback paths and recovery

We designed onchain recovery paths to work independently of us. Define who can use them and test them before activation.

During fail-open periods, mandatory assertion protection is reduced. A restrictive assertion may still block legitimate activity. See the [Enforcement FAQ on recovery](./faq#availability-false-positives-and-recovery).
