---
title: 'Evaluate Credible Layer'
description: 'A quick checklist for protocol teams and networks evaluating Credible Layer'
---

This checklist helps teams evaluate whether the Credible Layer is a fit for their protocol or network.

## For Protocol Teams

- Do we have invariants that should never be violated?
- Can we encode those rules as assertions in Solidity?
- Do we want pre-execution protection rather than post-mortem monitoring?
- Are we comfortable with transparent, public rules?
- Do we have a team to monitor invalidations and iterate on rules?
- Is our network and assertion specification supported by the intended deployment?
- If only some builders participate, can every protected path reach a reviewed on-chain gate?
- Can all relevant submitters use the required route?
- Have we accepted the fail-open, bypass, management, and recovery assumptions?

## For Networks & Sequencers

- Can we integrate an external enforcer into block building?
- Do we want a policy layer for transaction admission?
- Do we want to offer protocol teams optional enforcement?
- Do we have monitoring and invalidation review workflows?

## Decision Support

Treat network support, enforceable path coverage, authorization, and recovery as prerequisites rather than a score. If a protected operation can bypass the enforcement boundary, resolve that gap or explicitly narrow the claimed coverage.

Use the [FAQ](./faq) to review routing, availability, and policy questions before committing to a rollout.
