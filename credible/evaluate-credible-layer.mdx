title: 'Prepare for Your Integration'

---

Prepare your protocol context and the failure scenarios you want to address. Bring what you have. Our FDEs help define the policies and integration.

## Prerequisites

Identify a technical contact who understands the protocol and the operations you want to protect.

## 1. Describe the Protection Need

Describe the assets and operations to protect, the harmful outcomes to prevent, and gaps in existing controls. Include normal activity and exceptions that must remain possible, such as migrations or large withdrawals.

Our FDEs help turn these requirements into policies for your team to approve.

## 2. Gather Technical Context

Prepare the available information:

- Networks, contract addresses, source code, and architecture.
- Upgrade mechanisms, privileged roles, and dependencies.
- Systems that submit transactions and restrictions on changing their routes.
- Tests, audit findings, and examples of legitimate activity.
- Constraints on contract changes, governance timing, or availability.

Use the agreed private channel for proprietary materials. We do not need credentials or signing keys.

## 3. Identify the People and Decisions Needed

Identify who reviews policies, approves contract changes, signs deployments, and responds to incidents. Agree on the initial coverage and the evidence required before activation.

Our FDEs prepare and test the integration. Your team retains authority over its policies and protocol. See [Work with Phylax](./work-with-phylax#the-integration-process) for responsibilities.

## 4. Review Ethereum Integration Feasibility

Review [guard coverage, routing, and recovery](./dapp-integration) with our FDEs. An immutable contract without a suitable guard cannot gain Ethereum protection through assertion registration alone.

Record the required contract changes and any unresolved coverage gaps.

## Ready for the Discussion

[Contact Phylax](./work-with-phylax#start-a-conversation) when you can describe the protection need and identify a technical contact.

Infrastructure operators should use [Builder Integration Planning](./ethereum-builder-integration) or the [L2 guide](./l2-integrations/overview).
