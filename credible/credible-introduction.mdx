## Key Benefits of the Credible Layer

### For dApps

- **Network-Native, Rule-Based Security**: Every transaction that violates your rules is removed by the network at the sequencer level with zero false positives
- **Easy to Use**: Written in Solidity with foundry-like syntax, assertions require no contract modifications or downtime during implementation
- **Transparent Security**: All active assertions are public and verifiable by users and auditors

### For Networks

- **No Lock-In**: Runs natively as part of your network infrastructure with no external dependencies
- **No Throughput Degradation**: Assertions execute in parallel during sequencing without impacting block times
- **Attract Institutional Capital**: Reduce hack probability and provide auditable security rules for risk-averse institutions

### For Users

- **Visible Protection**: See exactly which protocols are protected and by what rules through the Transparency Dashboard
- **Provable Security**: Verify the specific security guarantees protecting your funds
- **Confidence**: Deploy capital knowing malicious transactions are prevented before they can execute
