## What is the Credible Layer?

Phylax checks protocol-defined rules, called assertions, before transactions are included. On an integrated rollup, those checks sit in the sequencer’s transaction-building path. On Ethereum, participating builders run the checks, while a contract guard makes selected actions depend on their marked blocks.

## Choose Your Enforcement Design

- **[Rollup sequencer integration](./network-integrations/rollup-sequencers)**: for supported deployments and covered paths, register assertions without adding a credible-block guard to your application. Eligible immutable contracts can be supported.
- **[Ethereum / participating builders](./network-integrations/architecture-ethereum)**: integrate a guard directly or through a supported hook/adapter, register active assertions, and use Credible RPC for simulation and private submission to Titan.

The difference is where enforcement authority comes from. A builder integration does not make other builders enforce assertions. See the [High-Level Overview](./credible-layer-overview) for the tradeoffs.
