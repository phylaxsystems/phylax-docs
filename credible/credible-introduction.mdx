## What is the Credible Layer?

The Credible Layer protects Ethereum contract operations through Solidity assertions, participating block builders, and on-chain guards. Builders exclude candidate transactions that violate active rules. Guards restrict protected execution to credible blocks while their gate is active, and the Credible RPC routes transactions to participating builders.

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Define rules in Solidity">
    Write assertions: Solidity rules that check the effects of transactions against your protocol’s requirements.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Participating builders validate transactions">
    Participating Ethereum builders run relevant assertions against candidate transactions before inclusion.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enforce the protected execution path">
    An on-chain guard restricts protected operations to credible blocks while its gate is active. Configure the wallet, executor, or service that submits the transaction to use the Credible RPC.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For Ethereum block building, start with [Architecture](./architecture-overview#decentralized-block-building). For routing, availability, and recovery questions, see the [FAQ](./faq).

## Key Benefits

- **Validation before inclusion** — Participating builders exclude transactions that violate active rules
- **Separate security policy** — Maintain assertions independently of protocol logic; guard requirements depend on the network integration
- **Reviewable Solidity** — Inspect the assertion source and release artifacts shared for your integration
- **Readable security posture** — Understand circuit breakers and verifiable operational risks through clear, plain-English breakdowns
- **Enforcement during block building** — Review the participating operators, guard configuration, and recovery assumptions in the [Trust Model](./trust-model)
- **Deterministic decisions** — Binary enforcement based on your rules, not opaque scoring

Sequencer-based enforcement and earlier network designs now live in [L2 and Sequencer Integrations](./l2-integrations/overview).
