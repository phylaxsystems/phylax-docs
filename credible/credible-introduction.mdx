Phylax helps Ethereum protocols enforce custom security policies before settlement. Policies are implemented as Solidity assertions, checked by participating builders, and paired with guards on protected operations.

Custom policies are developed with **Phylax sales engineering**. Together, you define the operations to protect, the legitimate behavior that must remain possible, and the integration and recovery requirements. You do not need to arrive with assertion code or install developer tools to start that conversation.

## What You Can Protect

Policies can constrain asset outflows, administrative changes, collateralization, oracle deviations, and relationships between balances or positions. The appropriate rules depend on your protocol and its normal operations.

For example, a rule that rejects every ownership change would also reject a planned transfer to a new governance contract. Policy design must account for legitimate exceptions as well as harmful behavior. The [Assertions Book](../assertions-book/assertions-book-intro) provides patterns to discuss with the team.

## How Ethereum Enforcement Works

Participating builders evaluate assertions against candidate transactions and exclude candidates that violate active production rules. An on-chain guard restricts protected operations to credible blocks while its gate is active. The Credible RPC routes submissions to participating builders.

Protection depends on the agreed policies, covered execution paths, deployed guards, and operational configuration. Learn about [Ethereum enforcement](./credible-layer-overview) and its [trust and recovery assumptions](./trust-model).

## From Evaluation to Operation

The integration starts with a coverage discussion. Your team and Phylax then define policies, review implementation and test evidence, prepare guards and transaction routing, and agree on activation and recovery procedures.

Your team provides protocol context and approves the intended behavior. Authorized protocol signers retain control of on-chain actions under the deployed permissions. Sales engineering guides custom policy development; the implementation and review responsibilities are agreed during scoping.

Use [Prepare for Your Integration](./evaluate-credible-layer) to gather the information for that discussion. The [FAQ](./faq) covers engineering effort, authority, availability, and policy changes.
