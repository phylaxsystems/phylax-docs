A lending market records more borrowed tokens than supplied tokens. This template rejects that aggregate state.

## Behavior

`TokensBorrowedInvariant` reads total supply from slot `0` and total borrowed from slot `1` at transaction end. It requires supply to be greater than or equal to borrowing.

## Configuration

Replace the slots and normalize both quantities to the same asset and scale. Receipt-token supply, indexed debt shares, and underlying assets are not interchangeable. Apply accrued indices consistently if the deployed protocol stores scaled balances.

## Scenarios

| Transaction | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Supply 1,000; borrowed 900 | Accepted |
| Supply 1,000; borrowed 1,001 | Rejected |
| Supply 1,000; borrowed 900; borrower collateral is worthless | Accepted by this aggregate bound |

## Coverage limits

The bound does not check collateral value, account health, interest accrual correctness, or available liquidity. It cannot establish solvency by itself. Use [operation-level lending checks](./reusable-patterns#post-operation-solvency) for borrower health.
