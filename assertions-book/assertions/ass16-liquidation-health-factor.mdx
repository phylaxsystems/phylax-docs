A liquidation should satisfy the protocol's eligibility and settlement rules. This legacy example intends to require positive repayment and seizure, pre-health at most 100, and post-health above pre-health and at least 120.

<Warning>
The reviewed upstream example needs correction before production use. The limitation below is present in the linked source; the imported code is preserved for comparison.
</Warning>

## Source limitation

The code creates pre- and post-call fork identifiers but reads `healthFactor` directly both times. Those reads are not bound to different snapshots, so they do not establish health improvement. The constants also use a custom scale of 100, not the usual `1e18`.

## Adaptation requirements

Use [PostOperationSolvencyAssertion](./reusable-patterns#post-operation-solvency) as the snapshot-aware starting point. Decode the liquidated account from the exact triggering call, read health at `_preCall(ctx.callStart)` and `_postCall(ctx.callEnd)`, and apply the protocol's scale and liquidation policy.

## Scenarios

| Scenario | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Health improves from 0.90 to 1.05 on a 1.0 threshold | Meets the newer pattern's improvement and restoration conditions |
| Health improves from 0.80 to 0.90 | Still fails a full-restoration policy |
| Collateral is seized without improving health | Should fail an improvement policy |

These are scenarios for the replacement policy, not claimed results of the legacy code below. Some protocols permit partial liquidations that remain unhealthy; others can change health discontinuously when debt reaches zero. Test those paths. Health improvement alone does not prove correct repayment, collateral pricing, or liquidation eligibility.
