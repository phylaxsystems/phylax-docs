A strategy harvest should retain or increase the vault's recorded balance and price per share. This example rejects a harvest that lowers either value.

## Behavior

`BeefyHarvestAssertion` registers the teaching interface `harvest(bool badHarvest)`. It compares balance slot `0` and price-per-share slot `1` across the matched call. Both post-call values must be greater than or equal to their pre-call values.

## Configuration

Replace the selector and storage reads with the deployed vault's ABI and accounting. The boolean argument is a mock control, not a production Beefy interface. Define whether harvest may realize losses, pay fees, or move assets into a strategy before choosing these inequalities.

## Scenarios

| Transaction | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Balance and price per share both increase | Accepted |
| Both remain unchanged | Accepted |
| Balance increases but price per share decreases | Rejected |
| Harvest reports a real strategy loss | Rejected under this policy |

## Coverage limits

Recorded values can be manipulated together. These checks do not independently value rewards or verify custody. If losses are a supported operation, use a bounded-loss or operation-specific policy instead of treating every decrease as an exploit.
