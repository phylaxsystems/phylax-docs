A lending deposit should increase a recorded aggregate by the deposited amount. This example checks that local accounting relationship for each covered call.

## Behavior

Despite its `PositionSumAssertion` name, the example does not sum user positions. It registers `deposit(address,uint256)` and requires the change in total supply at slot `0` across the matched call to equal the amount argument.

## Configuration

Use this only when the amount and aggregate are in the same units and deposits increase that aggregate one-for-one. Replace the selector, argument decoding, and storage slot for your contract. Share-based accounting needs the expected share conversion instead of a raw asset amount.

## Scenarios

| Transaction | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Deposit 100; aggregate increases by 100 | Accepted |
| Deposit 100; aggregate increases by 90 or 110 | Rejected |
| Aggregate increases by 100 without receiving tokens | Accepted by this accounting-only check |

## Coverage limits

This does not establish backing, reconcile all positions, or cover withdrawals. For independent asset and liability totals, see [accounting conservation](./reusable-patterns#accounting-conservation). For vault custody, use the [ERC-4626 components](./vault-policies#other-vault-components).
