A deposit mints too many shares, diluting existing holders. This template rejects a lower asset-per-share ratio after a deposit or withdrawal.

## Behavior

`ERC4626DepositWithdrawAssertion` registers standard `deposit(uint256,address)` and `withdraw(uint256,address,address)` selectors. With assets in slot `0` and supply in slot `1`, it requires `postAssets × preSupply >= preAssets × postSupply`. It skips the comparison when either share supply is zero.

## Configuration

Replace the toy slots with the vault's actual accounting. For real vaults, prefer the [reusable ERC-4626 policy](./vault-policies), which reads the interface at explicit snapshots, includes mint and redeem, and supports a tolerance.

## Scenarios

| Transaction | Result |
| --- | --- |
| 100 assets/100 shares becomes 110/110 | Accepted: ratio remains 1 |
| 100/100 becomes 110/120 | Rejected: existing shares are diluted |
| First deposit starts from zero supply | Ratio check is skipped |

## Coverage limits

The example has no rounding tolerance, omits mint and redeem, and uses potentially overflowing products. It permits share-price increases and does not prove that tokens reached the vault. Legitimate fees or loss recognition can lower the ratio; test those paths before applying a no-decrease policy.
