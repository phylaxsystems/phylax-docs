A borrow or collateral withdrawal can leave an account below the minimum health factor. This template checks a stored health factor after selected lending calls.

## Behavior

`LendingHealthFactorAssertion` registers supply, borrow, withdraw, and repay selectors with `(uint256 marketId, uint256 amount)` arguments. It reads a nested market/account mapping rooted at slot `0` and requires post-call health of at least `1e18`.

## Configuration

Replace the ABI, mapping layout, account selection, and scale. The current helper selects the first matching successful caller with a one-call limit; it does not resolve the caller by the triggering call ID. For batched or routed operations, use [PostOperationSolvencyAssertion](./reusable-patterns#post-operation-solvency), which matches `ctx.callStart`.

## Scenarios

| Transaction | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Covered call ends with health 1.10 × 10^18 | Accepted for the account selected by the template |
| Borrow ends with health 0.95 × 10^18 | Rejected |
| Partial repayment improves health from 0.80 to 0.90 × 10^18 | Rejected, although it improves the position |

## Coverage limits

A blanket health floor on repayment and supply can block recovery. Select risk-increasing operations deliberately. The toy mapping is not an Aave, Euler, or other production storage layout; adapt the check to the deployed implementation.
