A swap should not reduce the reserve product of a constant-product pool. This template compares the recorded reserve product before and after a transaction.

## Behavior

`ConstantProductAssertion` reads separate reserve slots `0` and `1` at transaction boundaries. It requires `postReserve0 × postReserve1 >= preReserve0 × preReserve1`.

## Configuration

Replace the slots and decode packed reserves if necessary. Real Uniswap V2 reserves do not occupy these two independent slots. The template runs at transaction end, so a deployment that permits liquidity removal needs operation-specific coverage or an explicit policy for those removals.

## Scenarios

| Transaction | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Reserves 100/100 become 90/112 | Accepted: product rises from 10,000 to 10,080 |
| Reserves 100/100 become 90/110 | Rejected: product falls to 9,900 |
| Proportional liquidity removal leaves 90/90 | Rejected, although it can be legitimate |

## Coverage limits

Stored reserves are not proof of token custody or a fair market price. A manipulated swap can preserve the product. Bound reserve values to avoid multiplication overflow, and test fee-on-transfer behavior.
