## Evidence required for a prevention claim

Record the source chain and block, affected contract revisions, assertion code and specification, CLI/runtime versions, covered call paths, replay result, and legitimate counterexamples. Then verify deployment routing and control authority. Passing a historical replay alone does not establish live protection.
