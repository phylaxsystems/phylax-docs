This case explains the failure mechanism and the policy boundary it suggests.

**Risk domain:** Accounting and rounding. **Evidence:** source review, November 17, 2026. **Policy maturity:** proposed; no pinned Phylax exploit replay is supplied here.

## What the source establishes

Compound Proposal 65 documents over-accrued COMP introduced by Proposal 62 and the corrective work following Proposal 64. The repair distinguishes unclaimed excess accrual from rewards already claimed. This page does not equate an exposed balance, an accrual error, and realized loss. [Compound Proposal 65](https://hackmd.io/@TylerEther/BkdJGdRVY).

## Candidate policy

Bound incremental rewards against configured emission speeds, elapsed blocks, market indices, and account balances at explicit snapshots. Test initialization at the exact index boundary.

## Coverage and limitations

A universal maximum reward amount rejects legitimate accumulated claims. Internal Solidity functions do not have external call frames merely because a selector is constructed for them; attach checks to actual reachable operations or a transaction boundary. Upgrade and correction transactions need explicit treatment.

A production claim also requires a pinned implementation, allowed-operation tests, a replay of the relevant failure, and verified trigger and enforcement coverage. A local example passing tests establishes only its stated fixture behavior. See [Testing Strategy](/credible/testing-strategy) and [Ethereum architecture](/credible/architecture-overview).
